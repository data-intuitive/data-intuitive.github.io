How we work with you

From one researcher to a global R&D organisation or CRO

Four main ways to work with us, depending on where you are today: run your own workflows on the platform, bring our bioinformaticians in for a project, or partner with us to deliver custom-made omics analysis to your own customers. Larger organisations can also deploy a private, enterprise-wide platform.

We handle the pipelines, you focus on the science

The platform takes care of all the repetitive workflow setup and processing, so you're not re-running the same steps on every dataset. You get clean, validated data ready for interpretation, and your team's time goes to the science that actually matters.

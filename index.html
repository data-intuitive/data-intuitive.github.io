Without the trade-off

Data and compute stay inside your security boundary.
Production-grade omics pipelines run on top, fast and consistent.

Runs in your environment

Your sequencing data never leaves your environment. No uploads, no transfers, no waiting on data-transfer approvals. We deliver the workflows; your data and compute stay with you.

Results you can stand behind

Same inputs, same pipeline version, same answer, months or years later. When a reviewer asks you to regenerate a figure, you get exactly what you had before.

Fast, validated runs

Start your analysis the moment your run finishes. No waiting in the core-facility queue, no asking a bioinformatician to re-build the pipeline for every project.

Single-digit euros per sample

Pay-as-you-go: you buy credits and spend them per run, not per sample. List price is around 26 euros per credit, and because each run processes many samples, routine assays work out to a few euros per sample. A single researcher can start small.

Respond to experiment questions

A few plain questions about your experiment, in biologist's terms rather than bioinformatics jargon, pick the right pipeline for you. No coding, no command line, no prior bioinformatics knowledge needed.

  • Experiment type: single-cell RNA-seq, bulk RNA-seq, spatial (Visium), proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics · with ATAC-seq, WGS, WES and ChIP-seq in development
  • Species and reference genome
  • Number of samples and batches
  • Comparing conditions (treatment vs control)?

We compose your data workflow

Validated modules compose into an executable pipeline. No new code, only tested pieces combined for your run.

Typically: minutes from your answers to a composed pipeline.

  • Selected from the validated module catalogue
  • Versioned and containerised
  • Nextflow orchestration generated automatically
  • Audit-ready by construction (report on demand)

Handover

We deliver the workflow

When a scientist clicks run, the platform sends a validated workflow to you. You can run it in your compute environment, easy. Your infrastructure runs it against your data. The workflow itself stays on the platform, where we maintain it.

Typically: immediately after the setup.

  • We maintain the workflow so you always run the current validated version, or one you pin.
  • One way of working across every assay – the foundation for multi-omics.

Your data

Nobody sees your data unless you decide they should

The platform configures the workflow with us. Your data and compute stay with you, in one of two environments.

Your own environment
Your cloud account or HPC cluster. Workflows run against your storage; nothing copies out.
A dedicated environment we set up
Your team or your CRO writes raw and ready-to-interpret data into it. You stay the owner.
Never on our infrastructure
Only configuration and validation artefacts pass through the platform.

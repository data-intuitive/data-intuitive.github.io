Reproducibility & Scientific Integrity

Ecosystem

Our Data Workflow Suite consists of the Viash Tool, the Viash Hub platform and an extensive repository of bioinformatics data workflows and modules: Viash Catalogue.

Viash Catalogue

The open-source Viash Catalogue offers a comprehensive collection of publicly available bioinformatics data workflows and modules. They are ready for stand-alone use or integration into larger data workflows.

Viash Hub

Viash Hub is a platform for managing, deploying, and sharing bioinformatics modules and workflows.

It allows users to publish, share, and maintain versioned modules, ensuring reusability and easy access following the FAIR principles.

Optimised for cloud and HPC environments, it simplifies launching data workflows.

Viash

Viash is the open-source tool developed by Data Intuitive to manage, deploy, and share data workflows. It automates the generation of modular, executable components, ensuring that workflows are reproducible, scalable, and easily integrated into various computational environments.

Engineered with deep expertise in Nextflow pipelines, Viash minimizes manual boilerplate coding, significantly boosting maintainability and reducing errors.

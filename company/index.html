Where we are, what we do

Built to run in production

We're headquartered in Flanders, Belgium, and work with customers around the world. We take bioinformatics tooling (open-source and proprietary alike) and build the layer that makes it run in production: maintained, reproducible and reliable in enterprise environments. Our work spans the full omics stack, from single-cell and bulk RNA-seq to spatial, proteomics and metabolomics, with infrastructure and software engineers making sure it holds up at scale.

What we believe

Four principles that shape the work

01

Open at the foundation

We author and maintain Viash, the open-source workflow framework that underpins our platform. The foundation belongs to the community.

02

Efficient and operational by default

Research-grade code proves a method works once. Production-grade infrastructure – efficient and operational – makes it work the hundredth time, on data it's never seen, in audits it has to pass.

03

Vendor-neutral by design

Your data and your compute are yours. The platform runs wherever you need it. No proprietary file formats, no forced cloud.

04

Insights that outlast the project

Work scientists can reuse. Pipelines that produce reproducible outputs two years on, on the same data, with the same parameters.
